# Data and licence

## Data

- `figures/*.json` and `results/*.json` hold the computed results behind the tables. They are produced by the searches described in `REPRO.md`.
- The audio realism pool uses ReelSynth factory wavetable periods and other license-clean sources. Each source and its licence is listed in `SAMPLE_LICENSES.md`.
- Synthetic CNC and PMU rows in the transfer tables are proxies where the KIT and IEEE DataPort sources are login-walled (see `REPRO.md`). The transfer boards are wrap-protocol stress tests, not diagnosis claims.
- No listening study was run (N_listeners = 0).
